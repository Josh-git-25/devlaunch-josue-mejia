What is bitcoin?

Bitcoin is a pioneering digital currency that has transformed the landscape of financial transactions since its inception in 2009. 
Created by an anonymous entity known as Satoshi Nakamoto, 
Bitcoin operates on a decentralized network, 
allowing users to engage in peer-to-peer transactions without the need for intermediaries such as banks. 
This revolutionary approach has not only introduced a new form of currency but has also sparked widespread interest in blockchain technology and cryptocurrencies.
